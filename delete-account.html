Delete your InvoiceMe account and data

Last updated: 25 November 2026

You can delete your InvoiceMe account and all the data synced to it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Share or export any invoices you want to keep first.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open InvoiceMe and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account & sync.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted from our servers straight away, and its data is removed from that phone.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

  1. Email InvoiceMe@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to InvoiceMe.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my InvoiceMe account".

We may reply to check the request came from you. We will delete your account and confirm within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in details (email address and hashed password).
  • Everything synced to your account: business profile and logo, customers, saved items, invoices, payments and settings.

What is kept

  • Nothing from your account is kept after deletion.
  • Short-term technical logs held by our hosting provider (such as IP addresses and request times) are removed automatically within a short period, as set by the provider.
  • Data on a phone where you used InvoiceMe without signing in stays on that phone until you delete the app.
  • PDFs or exports you already shared with others are outside our control.

Only want to delete some data?

You can delete single customers, items and draft invoices in the app without deleting your account. To ask us to delete specific data, email InvoiceMe@gmail.com.
