Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Invoicing Shed app uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have. It is written in plain English.

The Invoicing Shed ("we", "us") publishes the Invoicing Shed app. For the data in your Invoicing Shed account, we are the "controller" under UK data protection law. If you have a question, email theinvoicingshed@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your invoices, customers, items and business details are stored on your phone.
  • An account is optional. If you create one, we copy that data to our sync service, hosted in London, UK, so you can use it on more than one device.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking. We do not sell your data.
  • The app only asks for your photo library, and only when you choose a logo.
  • You can delete your account and all synced data from inside the app at any time.

What the app stores on your phone

Without an account, everything stays on your device, in a private database that only The Invoicing Shed can open. It holds:

  • Your business profile: name, description, address, phone, email, website, tax number, logo, colours, payment instructions, terms and any custom fields you add.
  • Your customers: the names, contact details and addresses you enter.
  • Your saved items: products and services, with prices and tax settings.
  • Your invoices: lines, totals, dates, notes, status, payments you record, and a fixed copy of each invoice from when it was sent.
  • App settings: currency, tax settings and invoice numbering.

We cannot see this data. It does not leave your phone unless you share something or sign in to an account.

Phone backups. On Android, The Invoicing Shed turns off automatic app backups, so this data is not copied to Google Drive. On iPhone and iPad, the database is part of your normal device backup (iCloud or your computer), which Apple protects under your Apple account.

Sign-in details. If you sign in, the app keeps your login token in the phone's secure keychain (iOS) or keystore (Android). It stays on that phone only and is not included in backups.

App lock

If you turn on App lock, the app asks your phone to check it is you, using Face ID, fingerprint or your phone passcode. Your phone does this check itself. The Invoicing Shed never receives or stores your face, fingerprint or passcode. It only saves whether App lock is on, in the phone's secure storage.

Sharing invoices, reminders and exports

When you share an invoice PDF, a payment reminder or a CSV export, the app opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes, for example Mail, Messages or a files app. The Invoicing Shed does not send anything to your customers itself. The app deletes old PDF and CSV copies from its temporary folder when it starts and before each new share.

Photo library

The app asks for your photo library only when you tap the button to add a business logo. It reads only the one image you pick. The logo is saved in your business profile and added to your invoices. The app does not use the camera or microphone, and it does not look at any other photos.

Optional account and sync

If you create an account, the app uses Supabase (supabase.com) to run sign-in and store your synced data. Supabase acts as our data processor: it only handles your data on our instructions. Our database is hosted in Supabase's London (UK) region.

What we store

  • Your email address and a securely hashed password. We never store your password in readable form.
  • A copy of your app data: business profile (including logo), customers, saved items, invoices and app settings, as listed above.
  • For deleted customers, only a small "deleted" note is kept so your other devices remove them too. Their details are wiped. For deleted invoices, only the invoice number is kept, so it is never reused.

Emails we send

When you create an account, Supabase sends you an email to confirm your address. We do not send marketing emails.

Technical logs

Like most online services, Supabase keeps short-term technical logs of requests, such as IP address, device type and time. These are used to run and protect the service, not for advertising.

Who can see it

Each account can only read its own records. The database enforces this, so other users cannot see your data. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Supabase staff may access systems only to run and support the service. Supabase is a US company, so in rare cases support access may come from outside the UK. When that happens it is covered by Supabase's data processing agreement and the UK's approved safeguards for international transfers.

Signing in and out

The first time you sign in on a phone, anything you created there before signing in is added to your account. When you sign out, the app syncs your changes and then removes that account's data from the phone. The data stays in your account until you delete it.

Why we use your data (legal basis)

  • To provide the app and your account (sign-in, sync, backup): this is needed to perform our agreement with you.
  • To keep the service secure (technical logs, abuse limits): our legitimate interest in running a safe service.

Information about your customers

Invoices contain details about your customers, such as names, emails and addresses. For that information you are in control: you decide what to enter and what to do with it, and you are responsible for having the right to use it. We store it only to provide the app to you, and we never contact your customers.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of your data.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: deleting the app removes all its data from the phone. You can also delete single customers, items and draft invoices in the app.
  • In your account: in the app, go to Settings > Account & sync > Delete account and confirm. This deletes your account, sign-in details and all synced data from our servers straight away. It cannot be undone.
  • Without the app: see Delete your account and data.

How long we keep data

We keep synced data for as long as your account exists, and delete it as soon as you delete your account. Supabase's technical logs are kept only for a short time, as set by Supabase.

Children

The Invoicing Shed is a business tool for adults. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (and the EU GDPR, if it applies to you) you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and you can object to how we use it. You can see and change most of it in the app. For anything else, email theinvoicingshed@gmail.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to help first.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
